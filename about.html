Hi, I'm Luka

a DP, Colorist, and Director based in Belgrade, Serbia, working on movies, commercials and music videos.

I started as an event and portrait photographer, but a gradual shift towards marketing and content creation led me to video. I started doing music videos for small artists, and eventually made my way to film sets. Even though I still direct some projects, I'm a DP with a love for coloring. All of the projects you see on my website are edited, shot and colored by me.
